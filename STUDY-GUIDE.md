# Your laptop study workspace

Open **Study arena**. Start with one course, upload a searchable PDF under **Your PDFs**, then add the chapters and sections covered by your exam. Each section has a title, an optional set of PDFs, a physical PDF page range and relative exam weight. Include material you have not mastered; leaving weak sections out inflates the usefulness of any readiness estimate.

The **You are here** card shows your selected chapter, section and page range. It is saved in your encrypted vault. Uploaded originals, extracted text, answers and practice history are encrypted there too. PDFs are limited to 20 MB, 500 pages and one million extracted characters. Split large books by chapter. Image-only/scanned PDFs need OCR elsewhere first. Equations, diagrams, tables and Arabic layouts may extract imperfectly; inspect the source before trusting a mathematical answer. This version does not visually understand PDF images.

## Ask your PDFs

Ask a question in the selected section. Retrieval searches only the chosen PDFs and pages, ranks matching text passages locally, and passes those passages to your local model. Returned reference IDs must correspond to real retrieved passages. You can inspect the source text and its physical PDF page number. PDF page 7 may not be printed textbook page 7.

No match produces an explicit message instead of a general-knowledge fallback. Lexical retrieval is private and needs no embedding model, but synonyms and follow-up phrases such as “explain that” may not match well. Use the actual topic terms or broaden the page range. Citations identify retrieved evidence; they do not prove that every generated sentence is correct.

## Practice and level-ups

Generate 3, 5 or 10 multiple-choice questions at foundation, application or exam-style difficulty. A local model writes the options, explanation and answer key using the selected PDF passages. The app validates their structure, references and option uniqueness, and shuffles answer positions. It marks your selected answer deterministically against that key. A generated key can still be wrong; flag an incorrect or ambiguous question to exclude it from scoring.

Practice mode shows explanations after the first answer. A source hint marks the answer as assisted. Timed mode disables hints and withholds solutions until you finish. Unanswered questions count as incorrect at finish. The timer begins when generation completes; it does not charge model generation time. Timed/closed-book practice is self-administered, not proctored. A session is saved, and you can resume it; the server enforces its original deadline.

### XP and player level

A first unassisted attempt earns 4 XP. A correct answer adds 6, 12 or 20 XP according to selected difficulty. An assisted attempt earns 2 XP. Exact question repeats, including shuffled-option repeats, earn no additional XP or readiness. Near-duplicates cannot reliably be detected. Player level is `1 + floor(sqrt(XP/50))`. XP stays even when evidence becomes old; it motivates practice and is not a measure of current exam readiness.

### Readiness levels

Each section uses its latest 30 independent, unassisted, unflagged answers within 30 days. A conservative accuracy statistic, the 80% Wilson lower confidence bound, becomes its score. Untested sections count as zero. Your exam weights determine the course average. Section coverage means at least five independent answers in that section. Difficulty is selected by you and generated by the model; it has not been externally calibrated.

| Level | Label | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Unassessed | No current independent evidence |
| 1 | Learning | Some independent answers |
| 2 | Building skill | 8 current answers, score ≥40, section coverage ≥50% |
| 3 | Exam practice | 20 answers, score ≥60, coverage ≥80%, 4 exam-level answers |
| 4 | Ready for a mock | 30 answers, score ≥75, all sections covered, 6 exam-level and 6 timed answers |
| 5 | Strong practice evidence | 50 answers, score ≥85, all sections covered, 12 exam-level and 12 timed answers |

These are transparent app thresholds, **not validated grade predictions**. The score does not know your actual exam blueprint unless you enter it, cannot verify closed-book conditions and cannot evaluate a written mathematical proof. Generated MCQs are useful practice, but they do not replace official past papers or handwritten problem solving. Readiness may decrease after mistakes or when evidence ages out. Editing a section's learning scope resets that section's current evidence; historical sessions and earned XP remain.

## Updates without losing memory

The installer places code under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SultanLocalAI\Application`. Your encrypted data stays separately in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SultanLocalAI\vault.sqlite3`. The desktop shortcut opens the stable launcher. Replacing code does not create a new vault or erase your memory. Use **Updates & backups → Create encrypted backup now** for a consistent snapshot; retain your passphrase.

The update mechanism accepts signed source releases from a pinned publisher, verifies their size and checksum, stages them, builds an isolated environment and tests imports before choosing the new code on restart. It backs up the encrypted database before activation and can select previous code. **A trusted release feed and signing key have not yet been hosted/configured.** The UI reports this and disables automatic checking until that is done. No claim is made that future features appear merely because a project exists on GitHub. Private repository release artifacts also need authenticated distribution; this initial updater supports direct public HTTPS code-only feeds, with signatures protecting authenticity. Your personal PDFs and vault never belong in that feed.
